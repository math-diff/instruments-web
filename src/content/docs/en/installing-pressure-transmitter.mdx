Correct installation is 80% of accurate pressure measurement. Follow these
steps for the DW-3051 smart transmitter.

## Mounting

- Mount the transmitter **below the tap** for liquids, **above** for gases and
  steam (to keep condensate in the leg).
- Use a 3-valve or 5-valve manifold to allow zeroing without breaking the
  process.
- Keep impulse lines **short and equal length** for differential measurement.

## Impulse lines

| Media | Tap location | Line fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquid | Below tap | Process liquid |
| Gas | Above tap | Dry |
| Steam | Above tap | Condensate |

## Wiring

1. Use shielded twisted pair, 0.5–1.5 mm².
2. Earth the shield at **one** end only (usually the power supply).
3. Observe polarity — the DW-3051 is non-polar but a reverse connection
   disables HART.
4. Loop supply: 12–45 V DC. Verify the supply can deliver (Vsupply −
   Vmin) ≥ 20 mA × Rload.

## Commissioning

After power-up, zero the transmitter at zero differential pressure using the
HART handheld or the local zero button. Then verify the 4 mA and 20 mA
endpoints against a reference.
