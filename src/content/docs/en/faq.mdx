## Selection

**What instruments do you manufacture?**

Three families: **pressure gauges** (Bourdon-tube gauges, stainless
gauges, capsule low-pressure gauges, ammonia-service gauges),
**temperature instruments** (bimetal thermometers, assembly and
mineral-insulated RTDs and thermocouples, including flameproof and
wear-resistant types), and **level instruments** (magnetic bypass
gauges, radar, float switches, glass-tube and flat-gauge sight
glasses). Each product page includes real photographs and parameters.

**How do I get a selection recommendation?**

Send via the [contact page](/en/contact): media name, operating range
(pressure / temperature / level), accuracy requirement, connection
standard and site conditions (vibration, hazardous-area class). Our
application engineers reply within one working day.

**Do you build custom variants?**

Yes. Common customizations: stem length, dial orientation (axial /
radial), liquid-filled damped cases, corrosion-resistant materials
(PTFE lining, Hastelloy), high-temperature construction and
hazardous-area protection. See delivery times below.

## Delivery and warranty

**What are the delivery times?**

- Standard models: 5–10 working days;
- Built-to-order configurations (special lengths, materials, dial
  orientation): 15–20 working days;
- Special materials and flameproof construction: 25–35 working days.

**What is the warranty?**

24 months from shipment. Instruments failing from non-human causes
within the warranty are repaired or replaced free of charge.

**Do you provide calibration certificates?**

Factory calibration reports are available. Pressure gauges used for
safety protection or custody transfer fall under mandatory periodic
verification — arrange testing with your local metrology authority;
we support with product documentation.

## Technical support

**Can a bimetal thermometer be adjusted in the field?**

The movement is calibrated at the factory and has **no field
adjustment**. Replace the instrument when it drifts beyond class.
Prevention is in the installation: adequate insertion depth and
vibration control — see the
[bimetal thermometer installation guide](/en/docs/installing-bimetal-thermometer).

**Do RTDs really need three-wire wiring?**

Use three-wire whenever leads exceed about one metre. In two-wire
connection, lead resistance adds directly to the error — 1 mm²
copper is ~0.18 Ω per 10 m, so a there-and-back pair adds ~0.36 Ω,
close to 1 °C of indication error, drifting with ambient temperature. See the
[RTD and thermocouple wiring guide](/en/docs/installing-rtd-thermocouple).

**Can a magnetic level gauge measure my media?**

Check three things first: media density (≥ 0.65 g/cm³ and stable at
operating conditions), corrosiveness (decides PTFE lining), and
crystallizing/fouling behavior — see the
[magnetic level gauge installation guide](/en/docs/installing-magnetic-level-gauge).
For viscous crystallizing media we recommend radar instead.

**What information do you need for hazardous-area instruments?**

Provide the zone classification (e.g. Zone 1 or 2), the gas group and
ignition temperature (or your area classification drawing). We verify
the protection type (Ex d / Ex ia) and temperature class against
GB/T 3836 and supply the certificate number.

## After-sales

**The instrument shows a fault — what do I do?**

Send the model, serial number and symptoms (photos help) via the
[contact page](/en/contact). Our service engineers give troubleshooting
advice first; confirmed quality issues are handled under warranty.

**Are spare parts available for discontinued models?**

Seals, wear parts and common modules are stocked for 10 years after
a model is discontinued. Provide the model and serial number when
inquiring.
