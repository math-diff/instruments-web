## Can I get a calibration certificate?

Yes. Every Premium-tier instrument ships with a traceable calibration
certificate. Essential and Standard tiers can add one at ordering.

## What is your lead time?

- Standard models: 5–10 working days.
- Configured-to-order: 15–20 working days.
- Custom materials (tantalum, Hastelloy): 25–35 working days.

## Do you support HART configuration tools?

Yes. Our transmitters are HART 7 compliant and work with any HART
handheld (e.g. Emerson 475, Meriam 415). A free configuration app is also
available on request.

## What warranty do you offer?

Standard 24 months from shipment, extended to 36 months for Premium tier
when registered online.

## Can I get spare parts?

Seals, manifolds and electronics modules are stocked for 10 years after
model discontinuation. Contact sales with your model serial.
