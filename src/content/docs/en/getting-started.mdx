Welcome to the Jingfeng Instruments documentation. This section helps you go from
"which instrument do I need?" to "how do I install and commission it?".

## What you'll find here

1. **Selection guides** — match a product family to your process.
2. **Installation manuals** — mounting, wiring and sealing.
3. **Commissioning** — calibration, range setting and protocol configuration.
4. **FAQ** — common field questions.

## How to read our datasheets

Every datasheet has the same structure:

- **Specifications** — the guaranteed limits.
- **Ordering code** — the model string that defines your exact unit.
- **Dimensions** — mounting face and electrical connections.
- **Certifications** — the markings carried on the nameplate.

## Place an inquiry

Send your process data (media, range, temperature, accuracy need, protocol)
through the [contact form](/contact) and an application engineer will respond
within one business day.
