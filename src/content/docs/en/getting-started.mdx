## How to write a good inquiry

The more complete the inquiry, the more accurate the selection.
Minimum information set:

1. **Media name** (or at least the media family and corrosiveness);
2. **Operating range**: normal + maximum pressure for gauges;
   temperature range for thermometers; level range and media density
   for level instruments;
3. **Connection standard**: thread specification or flange standard
   (e.g. DN25 PN1.0);
4. **Site conditions**: vibration, hazardous-area classification
   (zone + gas group), space restrictions;
5. **Accuracy and functions**: accuracy class, need for transmission
   or alarm switches.
