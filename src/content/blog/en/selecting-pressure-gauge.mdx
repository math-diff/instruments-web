A useful pressure gauge enquiry starts with the process: normal pressure, possible peaks, the medium, temperature and installation conditions. A photographed gauge is a starting point for a discussion; its appearance does not establish every material, operating limit or order option.

This guide helps prepare the information needed for a configuration quotation. Final suitability must be confirmed against the ordered instrument and its documentation.

## 1. Define the operating pressure and loading

Record minimum, normal and maximum pressure, including vacuum, pressure cycling and short surges. Separate normal operation from commissioning, cleaning and abnormal events. State the pressure units.

Choose a scale that is legible at the readings you need and remains within the permitted steady and fluctuating loads for that configuration. A percentage of span alone cannot establish suitability. For example, the [WIKA 232.50/233.50 data sheet](https://www.wika.com/media/Data-sheets/Pressure/Pressure-gauges/ds_pm0202_en_co.pdf) specifies configuration-dependent operating conditions. Its ratings apply to those WIKA instruments and do not establish Jingfeng product limits.

The [JF-1541 Bourdon-tube gauge](/en/products/pressure-gauge) is photographed with a 0–1.6 MPa scale. Confirm the required range and permissible loads before using that sample as a replacement specification.

## 2. State the required accuracy and its reference conditions

Specify the permitted error at the readings that matter, the applicable accuracy standard and whether calibration evidence is required. Where a specification defines error as a percentage of span, calculate it from the full span rather than the current reading.

For an arithmetic illustration, 1.6% of a 1.6 MPa span is 0.0256 MPa. This does not include temperature effects, reading error or calibration uncertainty and does not certify a product. The [stainless-case JF-1545](/en/products/stainless-pressure-gauge) has a visible 316L marking on its lower assembly. That marking does not identify every wetted part. Specify the exact medium, concentration, contaminants and cleaning fluids so the sensing element, connector and seals can be reviewed together.

For low gas pressure, consider a [capsule-element enquiry for JF-1543](/en/products/capsule-pressure-gauge), with gas compatibility, overload limits and mounting confirmed. For an [ammonia-system enquiry for JF-1542](/en/products/ammonia-pressure-gauge), identify the refrigerant and confirm every wetted material and seal. A general product name does not establish suitability for a particular medium.

## 4. Describe vibration, pulsation and temperature separately

Mechanical vibration and pressure pulsation are different loading conditions. Describe the source, frequency and severity as far as known rather than asking only for a vibration-resistant gauge.

Ask whether damping, a restrictor, isolation or remote mounting is appropriate. Confirm the effect of any accessory on response, blockage risk and service limits. Case filling alone is not evidence of overpressure protection. Check both process and ambient temperature limits for the selected construction and any proposed heat-isolation arrangement.

## 5. Specify the connection and installation dimensions

Give the thread standard, size, male or female end, sealing interface, connection position and mounting arrangement. For flanges, include the standard, size, pressure rating and facing. Obtain a dimensioned drawing when replacing an existing instrument.

The [Swagelok thread and end connection identification guide](https://www.swagelok.com/downloads/webcatalogs/EN/ms-13-77.pdf) explains how to identify connection systems. Use it as an identification reference, then verify the actual connection drawing for the gauge being ordered.

## Prepare the enquiry

- Medium and composition; normal, maximum and transient pressure.
- Process and ambient temperature; vibration and pulsation.
- Required scale, units, accuracy standard and calibration documentation.
- Connection, sealing method, dial diameter, mounting and a drawing where available.
- Quantity, destination and required project documents.

Compare the [pressure gauge families](/en/pressure-gauges), then [send your process requirements](/en/contact). Requested configurations, documentation and supply terms are confirmed in the quotation.
